# Gen I Labs Website 7-Day Launch Experiment Plan

**Status:** launch-week campaign plan for Umami-monitored distribution.
**Production website:** https://www.genilabs.co.za
**Primary conversion:** `outbound_click_calendly`
**Secondary conversion:** lead leak audit CTA clicks
**Scope:** public website launch tracking only.

## 1. Launch-Week Rules

- Use one UTM link per post/channel/message type.
- Do not mix campaign angles in one post.
- Judge each experiment by events, not vanity impressions alone.
- Do not include private prospect names or private lead data in UTM values.
- Do not treat `outbound_click_calendly` as a booked meeting until Calendly is connected later.
- Do not automate CRM/n8n/Twenty actions during this experiment week.

**Target audience:** real estate agency owners, principals, boutique agency founders, operators.


- `cta_click_lead_leak_audit_hero`

## 11. Launch-Week Success Thresholds

Early thresholds should be directional, not absolute, because traffic volume may be low.

- Any Calendly outbound click from a qualified source is meaningful.
- Any source with repeat audit CTA clicks deserves a second campaign angle.
- High product/trust interest but low Calendly suggests the offer needs clarification.
- High bounce with weak events suggests message-channel mismatch.

## 12. Not Yet Implemented

- No automated campaign publishing.
- No CRM integration.
- No booked-meeting tracking.
- No daily automated alerts.
- No private visitor identification.

## 13. Privacy / Safety Boundary

Do not paste private outreach message contents, prospect identities, or lead details into analytics reports. Umami should remain aggregate campaign/source tracking only.
